# Sources

Every exam fact in these notes comes from the sources below, all checked on 24 November 2026. If CompTIA changes a page after that date, CompTIA's page wins.

## CompTIA exam pages

- [CompTIA Network+ (N10-009)](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/network/): exam code, launch date, estimated retirement, maximum questions, question types, length, passing score and scale, recommended experience, languages, job roles, NICE and DoD 8140 work roles, domain weights.
- [CompTIA Security+ V7](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v7/): the recommended experience for Security+ (Network+ plus two years in a security or systems administrator role), used in the FAQ.

## Objectives document

- [CompTIA Network+ N10-009 V9 exam objectives, document version 6.0](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/35a7403ab73211ef9dcda6f347fbf652): every domain, objective and example list used to build the domain guides, the note that example lists are not exhaustive, the troubleshooting methodology steps, the port table, the acronym list used for the glossary, and the authorized materials use (brain dump) policy.

## CompTIA policies and help pages

- [How Are CompTIA Exams Scored?](https://help.comptia.org/hc/en-us/articles/11186025660308-How-Are-CompTIA-Exams-Scored): scaled scores, what CompTIA does not disclose, and what the score report contains.
- [CompTIA Certification Retake Policy](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/test-policies/comptia-certification-retake-policy/): waiting periods, paying for each attempt, retaking a passed exam.
- [Candidate testing policies](https://www.comptia.org/en/resources/test-policies/candidate-testing-policies/): online testing (recording, no scheduled breaks, rescheduling until start time, no shared devices) and score reports.
- [Earn continuing education units](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/ce/learn/earn-continuing-education-units-ceus/): three-year renewal cycle and CEUs required (A+ 20, Network+ 30, Security+ 50).

## Search data

- The FAQ questions come from Google "People also ask" results for CompTIA Network+ searches, including "network+ n10-009" (United States, English), collected through DataForSEO on 24 November 2026.

## What is our own guidance

The following are our advice, not CompTIA statements: the estimated number of questions per domain (weight times 90, rounded down), the pacing plan, the methods and traps on each domain page, the study plans, practice score targets, study-time estimates, and opinions on difficulty, job prospects and whether the certification is worth it.

## Practice items

All practice and diagnostic items are original, written for these notes. None are taken from CompTIA's exams or materials or from any prep provider.
